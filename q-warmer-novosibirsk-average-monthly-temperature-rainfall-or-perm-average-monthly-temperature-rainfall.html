Which is warmer, Novosibirsk or Perm?

Novosibirsk is 2.8 C colder in December to February than Perm.

Updated: 2026-10-19

The short answer

  • Novosibirsk is 2.8 C colder in December to February than Perm.
  • Novosibirsk is 1.9 C warmer in June to August.
  • Perm gets 261 mm more rain a year.
  • Novosibirsk has the larger seasonal swing, 5.2 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
