This page is a single place to check Jeonju climate: monthly averages. Jeonju runs from -1.0 C in Jan to 25.8 C in Aug, a swing of 26.8 C. What the Jeonju climate table shows

Values are multi-decadal climatological averages for the 35.82194, 127.14889 grid cell, not a single year. Rain is the daily mean, so a value of 2.00 mm/day is about 60 mm across a 30-day month. Averages smooth over extremes, so use them for planning rather than for what to pack for next Tuesday.
