Which is warmer, Al Hillah or Akure?

Al Hillah is 13.4 C colder in December to February than Akure.

Updated: 2026-10-04

The short answer

  • Al Hillah is 13.4 C colder in December to February than Akure.
  • Al Hillah is 13.0 C warmer in June to August.

  • Al Hillah has the larger seasonal swing, 25.5 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
