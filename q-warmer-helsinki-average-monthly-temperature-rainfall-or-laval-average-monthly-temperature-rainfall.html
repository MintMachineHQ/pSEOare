Which is warmer, Helsinki or Laval?

Helsinki is 6.8 C warmer in December to February than Laval.

Updated: 2026-10-22

The short answer

  • Helsinki is 6.8 C warmer in December to February than Laval.
  • Helsinki is 4.3 C colder in June to August.
  • Laval gets 373 mm more rain a year.
  • Laval has the larger seasonal swing, 11.4 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
