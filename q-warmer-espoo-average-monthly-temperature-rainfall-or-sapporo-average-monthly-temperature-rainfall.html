Which is warmer, Espoo or Sapporo?

Espoo is 3.4 C warmer in December to February than Sapporo.

Updated: 2026-10-27

The short answer

  • Espoo is 3.4 C warmer in December to February than Sapporo.
  • Espoo is 1.8 C colder in June to August.
  • Sapporo gets 545 mm more rain a year.
  • Sapporo has the larger seasonal swing, 5.2 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
