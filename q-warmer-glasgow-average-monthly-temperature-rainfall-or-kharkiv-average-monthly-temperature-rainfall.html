Which is warmer, Glasgow or Kharkiv?

Glasgow is 9.0 C warmer in December to February than Kharkiv.

Updated: 2026-10-19

The short answer

  • Glasgow is 9.0 C warmer in December to February than Kharkiv.
  • Glasgow is 8.2 C colder in June to August.
  • Glasgow gets 795 mm more rain a year.
  • Kharkiv has the larger seasonal swing, 19.0 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
