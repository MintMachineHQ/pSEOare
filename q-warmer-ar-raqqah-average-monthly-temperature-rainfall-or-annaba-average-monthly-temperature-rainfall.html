Which is warmer, Ar Raqqah or Annaba?

Ar Raqqah is 6.2 C colder in December to February than Annaba.

Updated: 2026-10-05

The short answer

  • Ar Raqqah is 6.2 C colder in December to February than Annaba.
  • Ar Raqqah is 5.0 C warmer in June to August.
  • Annaba gets 390 mm more rain a year.
  • Ar Raqqah has the larger seasonal swing, 11.8 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
