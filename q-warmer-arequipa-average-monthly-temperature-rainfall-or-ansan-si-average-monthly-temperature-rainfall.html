Which is warmer, Arequipa or Ansan-si?

Arequipa is 13.4 C warmer in December to February than Ansan-si.

Updated: 2026-10-08

The short answer

  • Arequipa is 13.4 C warmer in December to February than Ansan-si.
  • Arequipa is 13.5 C colder in June to August.
  • Ansan-si gets 1,081 mm more rain a year.
  • Ansan-si has the larger seasonal swing, 25.5 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
