Which is warmer, Gatineau or Minsk?

Gatineau is 3.1 C colder in December to February than Minsk.

Updated: 2026-10-19

The short answer

  • Gatineau is 3.1 C colder in December to February than Minsk.
  • Gatineau is 2.0 C warmer in June to August.
  • Gatineau has the larger seasonal swing, 5.6 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
