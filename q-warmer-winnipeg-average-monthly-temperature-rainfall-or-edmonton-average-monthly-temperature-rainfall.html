Which is warmer, Winnipeg or Edmonton?

Winnipeg is 5.6 C colder in December to February than Edmonton.

Updated: 2026-10-23

The short answer

  • Winnipeg is 5.6 C colder in December to February than Edmonton.
  • Winnipeg is 3.2 C warmer in June to August.
  • Winnipeg has the larger seasonal swing, 10.2 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
