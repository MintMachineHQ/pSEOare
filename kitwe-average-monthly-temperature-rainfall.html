This page is a single place to check Kitwe climate: monthly averages. Kitwe runs from 17.2 C in Jun to 26.6 C in Oct, a swing of 9.4 C. What the Kitwe climate table shows

Values are multi-decadal climatological averages for the -12.8024, 28.2132 grid cell, not a single year. Rain is the daily mean, so a value of 2.00 mm/day is about 60 mm across a 30-day month. Averages smooth over extremes, so use them for planning rather than for what to pack for next Tuesday.
