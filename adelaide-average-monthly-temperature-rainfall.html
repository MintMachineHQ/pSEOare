Extreme records include a high of **45.1°C** and a low of **-0.3°C**. The least rain typically falls in February, with averages well below the annual mean of 1.18 mm/day. Yes, light frost occurs in winter, though sub-zero temperatures are rare, with the record low at **-0.3°C**.
