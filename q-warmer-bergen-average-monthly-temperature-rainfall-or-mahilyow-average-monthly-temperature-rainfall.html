Which is warmer, Bergen or Mahilyow?

Bergen is 5.4 C warmer in December to February than Mahilyow.

Updated: 2026-10-26

The short answer

  • Bergen is 5.4 C warmer in December to February than Mahilyow.
  • Bergen is 4.7 C colder in June to August.
  • Bergen gets 1,965 mm more rain a year.
  • Mahilyow has the larger seasonal swing, 11.3 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
