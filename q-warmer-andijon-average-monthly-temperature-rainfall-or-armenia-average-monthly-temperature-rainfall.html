Which is warmer, Andijon or Armenia?

Andijon is 12.8 C colder in December to February than Armenia.

Updated: 2026-10-07

The short answer

  • Andijon is 12.8 C colder in December to February than Armenia.
  • Andijon is 12.9 C warmer in June to August.
  • Armenia gets 1,383 mm more rain a year.
  • Andijon has the larger seasonal swing, 26.3 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
