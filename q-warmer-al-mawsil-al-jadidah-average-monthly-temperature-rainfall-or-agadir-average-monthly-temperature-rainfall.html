Which is warmer, Al Mawsil al Jadidah or Agadir?

Al Mawsil al Jadidah is 7.2 C colder in December to February than Agadir.

Updated: 2026-10-04

The short answer

  • Al Mawsil al Jadidah is 7.2 C colder in December to February than Agadir.
  • Al Mawsil al Jadidah is 5.8 C warmer in June to August.
  • Al Mawsil al Jadidah has the larger seasonal swing, 13.6 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
