Which is warmer, Al Hasakah or Adana?

Al Hasakah is 2.8 C colder in December to February than Adana.

Updated: 2026-10-10

The short answer

  • Al Hasakah is 2.8 C colder in December to February than Adana.
  • Al Hasakah is 3.1 C warmer in June to August.
  • Adana gets 417 mm more rain a year.
  • Al Hasakah has the larger seasonal swing, 6.1 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
