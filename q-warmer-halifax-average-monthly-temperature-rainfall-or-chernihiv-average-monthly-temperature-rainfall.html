Which is warmer, Halifax or Chernihiv?

Halifax is 4.1 C warmer in December to February than Chernihiv.

Updated: 2026-10-19

The short answer

  • Halifax is 4.1 C warmer in December to February than Chernihiv.
  • Halifax is 3.6 C colder in June to August.
  • Halifax gets 673 mm more rain a year.
  • Chernihiv has the larger seasonal swing, 6.8 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
