Which is warmer, Antofagasta or Aleppo?

Antofagasta is 13.3 C warmer in December to February than Aleppo.

Updated: 2026-10-10

The short answer

  • Antofagasta is 13.3 C warmer in December to February than Aleppo.
  • Antofagasta is 13.0 C colder in June to August.
  • Aleppo gets 425 mm more rain a year.
  • Aleppo has the larger seasonal swing, 16.5 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
