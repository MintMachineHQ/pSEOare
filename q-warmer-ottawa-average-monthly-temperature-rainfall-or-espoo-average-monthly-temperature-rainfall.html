Which is warmer, Ottawa or Espoo?

Ottawa is 6.8 C colder in December to February than Espoo.

Updated: 2026-10-21

The short answer

  • Ottawa is 6.8 C colder in December to February than Espoo.
  • Ottawa is 3.3 C warmer in June to August.
  • Ottawa gets 254 mm more rain a year.
  • Ottawa has the larger seasonal swing, 10.3 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
