Which is warmer, Aktobe or Ankara?

Aktobe is 12.3 C colder in December to February than Ankara.

Updated: 2026-10-04

The short answer

  • Aktobe is 12.3 C colder in December to February than Ankara.
  • Aktobe is 0.7 C warmer in June to August.
  • Aktobe has the larger seasonal swing, 13.4 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
