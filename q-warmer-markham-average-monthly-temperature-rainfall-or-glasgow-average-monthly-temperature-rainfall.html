Which is warmer, Markham or Glasgow?

Markham is 8.2 C colder in December to February than Glasgow.

Updated: 2026-10-19

The short answer

  • Markham is 8.2 C colder in December to February than Glasgow.
  • Markham is 7.4 C warmer in June to August.
  • Glasgow gets 656 mm more rain a year.
  • Markham has the larger seasonal swing, 17.6 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
