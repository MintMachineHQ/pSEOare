Which is warmer, Bafoussam or Agadir?

Bafoussam is 6.3 C warmer in December to February than Agadir.

Updated: 2026-10-09

The short answer

  • Bafoussam is 6.3 C warmer in December to February than Agadir.
  • Bafoussam is 7.5 C colder in June to August.
  • Bafoussam gets 1,695 mm more rain a year.
  • Agadir has the larger seasonal swing, 13.5 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
