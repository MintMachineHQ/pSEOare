Kuala Terengganu climate: monthly averages

This page is a single place to check Kuala Terengganu climate: monthly averages. Kuala runs from 25.8 C in Jan to 28.6 C in May, a swing of 2.8 C. Rain is strongly seasonal: Dec is the wettest month at 19.65 mm/day, while Feb runs at 2.99 mm/day, about 6.6x drier. Taken together that is roughly 2,843 mm of rain a year for the grid cell. The build runs on a schedule and caches the last good response, so the numbers stay put even when the upstream API is having a bad day. What the Kuala Terengganu climate table shows

Values are multi-decadal climatological averages for the 5.3302, 103.1408 grid cell, not a single year. Rain is the daily mean, so a value of 2.00 mm/day is about 60 mm across a 30-day month. Averages smooth over extremes, so use them for planning rather than for what to pack for next Tuesday.
