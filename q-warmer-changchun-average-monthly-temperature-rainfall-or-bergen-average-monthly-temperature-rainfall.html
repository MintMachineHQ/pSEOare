Which is warmer, Changchun or Bergen?

Changchun is 12.4 C colder in December to February than Bergen.

Updated: 2026-10-19

The short answer

  • Changchun is 12.4 C colder in December to February than Bergen.
  • Changchun is 9.0 C warmer in June to August.
  • Bergen gets 2,087 mm more rain a year.
  • Changchun has the larger seasonal swing, 23.9 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
