Which is warmer, Ankara or Antwerp?

Ankara is 3.7 C colder in December to February than Antwerp.

Updated: 2026-10-06

The short answer

  • Ankara is 3.7 C colder in December to February than Antwerp.
  • Ankara is 3.3 C warmer in June to August.
  • Antwerp gets 344 mm more rain a year.
  • Ankara has the larger seasonal swing, 8.6 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
