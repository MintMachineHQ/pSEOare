Which is warmer, Vilnius or Vladivostok?

Vilnius is 2.7 C warmer in December to February than Vladivostok.

Updated: 2026-10-19

The short answer

  • Vilnius is 2.7 C warmer in December to February than Vladivostok.
  • Vilnius is 1.3 C colder in June to August.
  • Vladivostok has the larger seasonal swing, 5.9 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
