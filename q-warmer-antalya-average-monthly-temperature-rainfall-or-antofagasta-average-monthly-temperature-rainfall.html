Which is warmer, Antalya or Antofagasta?

Antalya is 12.4 C colder in December to February than Antofagasta.

Updated: 2026-10-04

The short answer

  • Antalya is 12.4 C colder in December to February than Antofagasta.
  • Antalya is 12.1 C warmer in June to August.
  • Antalya gets 818 mm more rain a year.
  • Antalya has the larger seasonal swing, 15.2 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
