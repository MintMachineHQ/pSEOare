Which is warmer, Athens or Avellaneda?

Athens is 14.1 C colder in December to February than Avellaneda.

Updated: 2026-10-09

The short answer

  • Athens is 14.1 C colder in December to February than Avellaneda.
  • Athens is 14.7 C warmer in June to August.
  • Avellaneda gets 603 mm more rain a year.
  • Athens has the larger seasonal swing, 4.6 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
