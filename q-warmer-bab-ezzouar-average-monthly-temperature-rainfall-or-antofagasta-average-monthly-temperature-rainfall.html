Which is warmer, Bab Ezzouar or Antofagasta?

Bab Ezzouar is 10.8 C colder in December to February than Antofagasta.

Updated: 2026-10-09

The short answer

  • Bab Ezzouar is 10.8 C colder in December to February than Antofagasta.
  • Bab Ezzouar is 10.9 C warmer in June to August.
  • Bab Ezzouar gets 634 mm more rain a year.
  • Bab Ezzouar has the larger seasonal swing, 11.5 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
