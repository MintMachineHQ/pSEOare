Which is warmer, Bialystok or La Paz?

Bialystok is 12.7 C colder in December to February than La Paz.

Updated: 2026-10-19

The short answer

  • Bialystok is 12.7 C colder in December to February than La Paz.
  • Bialystok is 13.1 C warmer in June to August.
  • Bialystok has the larger seasonal swing, 20.9 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
