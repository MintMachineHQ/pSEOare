Balbala climate: monthly averages

This page is a single place to check Balbala climate: monthly averages. Balbala runs from 24.3 C in Jan to 34.4 C in Jul, a swing of 10.1 C. Rain is strongly seasonal: Apr is the wettest month at 1.69 mm/day, while Jan runs at 0.18 mm/day, about 9.4x drier. Taken together that is roughly 235 mm of rain a year for the grid cell. What the Balbala climate table shows
