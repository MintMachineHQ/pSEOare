What the Trujillo climate table shows

Frequently asked questions

Why do the numbers differ from a weather app?

An app shows one location on one day. These are long-run averages, so they smooth out single hot or cold spells.

How much rain is 1.12 mm/day in a month?

Rain is recorded as a daily mean, so 1.12 mm/day is roughly 34 mm across a 30-day month.
