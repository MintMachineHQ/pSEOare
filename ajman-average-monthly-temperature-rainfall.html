Ajman’s climate features a hot, arid profile with minimal rainfall, averaging **28.6°C annually**, peaking in July at **35.7°C** and dipping to **20.1°C** in January. The emirate receives just **88 mm of rain yearly**, concentrated in January (0.74 mm/day). Extreme temperatures range from **10.9°C** to **46.1°C**, reflecting its desert-influenced weather.

What the Ajman climate table shows
