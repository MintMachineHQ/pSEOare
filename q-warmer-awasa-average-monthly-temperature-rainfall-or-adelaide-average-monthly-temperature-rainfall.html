Which is warmer, Awasa or Adelaide?

Awasa is 5.6 C colder in December to February than Adelaide.

Updated: 2026-10-08

The short answer

  • Awasa is 5.6 C colder in December to February than Adelaide.
  • Awasa is 4.5 C warmer in June to August.
  • Awasa gets 997 mm more rain a year.
  • Adelaide has the larger seasonal swing, 9.5 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
