Baghdad’s climate averages **23.9°C annually**, with July as the warmest month at **37.3°C** and January the coldest at **9.2°C**, alongside roughly **201 mm of rain per year**. The city experiences extreme seasonal swings, from scorching summers to mild winters, and receives most precipitation in January (about **1.18 mm/day**). Record temperatures span from a high of **52.6°C** to a low of **-5.0°C**, reflecting its arid, continental climate.
