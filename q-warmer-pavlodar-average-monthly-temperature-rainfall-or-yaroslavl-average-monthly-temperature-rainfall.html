Which is warmer, Pavlodar or Yaroslavl?

Pavlodar is 5.5 C colder in December to February than Yaroslavl.

Updated: 2026-10-29

The short answer

  • Pavlodar is 5.5 C colder in December to February than Yaroslavl.
  • Pavlodar is 3.8 C warmer in June to August.
  • Yaroslavl gets 421 mm more rain a year.
  • Pavlodar has the larger seasonal swing, 9.8 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
