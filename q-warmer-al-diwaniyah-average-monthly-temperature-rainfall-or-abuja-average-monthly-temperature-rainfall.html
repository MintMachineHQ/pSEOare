Which is warmer, Al Diwaniyah or Abuja?

Al Diwaniyah is 13.8 C colder in December to February than Abuja.

Updated: 2026-10-04

The short answer

  • Al Diwaniyah is 13.8 C colder in December to February than Abuja.
  • Al Diwaniyah is 13.0 C warmer in June to August.
  • Abuja gets 1,030 mm more rain a year.
  • Al Diwaniyah has the larger seasonal swing, 23.7 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
