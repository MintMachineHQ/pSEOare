Which is warmer, Adana or Al Mawsil al Jadidah?

Adana is 1.8 C warmer in December to February than Al Mawsil al Jadidah.

Updated: 2026-10-05

The short answer

  • Adana is 1.8 C warmer in December to February than Al Mawsil al Jadidah.
  • Adana is 4.8 C colder in June to August.
  • Adana gets 321 mm more rain a year.
  • Al Mawsil al Jadidah has the larger seasonal swing, 6.8 C more between its warmest and coldest month.
